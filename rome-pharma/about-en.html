About Us

Rome Pharma, part of Rome Group, focuses on helping international brands access the Turkish pharmaceutical market.

  • 3

    Offices

    Ankara, Dubai and the USA

  • 2

    Business lines

    Pharmaceutical supply and consulting

  • 3

    Access channels

    Direct supply, tenders, named patient programs

Two business lines

We combine supply and consulting so international pharmaceutical companies can reach patients in Türkiye.

  • Pharmaceutical supply

    Supplying pharmaceutical products for direct supply programs, government tenders and prescription-based programs (Named Patient Program)

  • Consulting and local representation

    Consultancy and local representation for international companies planning to enter the Turkish pharmaceutical market

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the first address for international companies seeking cooperation in the Turkish pharmaceutical market and to increase patients' access to new original medicines and better generics.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create synergy by building sustainable relationships between international companies supplying pharmaceuticals in Türkiye and local institutions and pharmaceutical suppliers.

How we work

Bringing your product to patients in Türkiye takes four steps, and we are with you at each one.

  1. 1

    Discovery and analysis

    We assess your product, your goals and the need in Türkiye together.

  2. 2

    Access roadmap

    We choose the best route among registration, named patient programs and tenders.

  3. 3

    Supply and tenders

    We deliver compliant, uninterrupted supply to public institutions and hospitals.

  4. 4

    Local representation

    As your local partner in Türkiye, we manage the process for the long term.

Our values

  • Reliability

    We deliver on our commitments, on time and in full.

  • Compliance

    Every step follows Turkish health regulations.

  • Transparency

    We keep our partners informed at every stage.

  • Patient focus

    We never forget there is a patient behind every decision.

Why Rome Pharma?

Rome Pharma collaborates with leading pharmaceutical companies around the world. Thanks to our reliability and professionalism, we help international companies enter the Turkish market smoothly.
