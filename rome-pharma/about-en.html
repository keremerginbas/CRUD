About Us

We become the hope of patients by facilitating access to innovative medicines.

Rome Pharma, as part of Rome Group, is a young and dynamic company focused on facilitating international brands' access to the Turkish pharmaceutical market.

Rome Pharma has two main business lines:

  • Supplying pharmaceutical products for direct supply programs, government tenders and prescription-based programs (Named Patient Program)
  • Consultancy and local representation for international companies planning to enter the Turkish pharmaceutical market

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the first address for international companies seeking cooperation in the Turkish pharmaceutical market and to increase patients' access to new original medicines and better generics.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create synergy by building sustainable relationships between international companies supplying pharmaceuticals in Türkiye and local institutions and pharmaceutical suppliers.

Why Rome Pharma?

Rome Pharma collaborates with leading pharmaceutical companies around the world. Thanks to our reliability and professionalism, we help international companies enter the Turkish market smoothly.
