One partner for entering Türkiye and reaching patients

We build the commercial, regulatory and institutional bridge an international manufacturer needs to bring its product to patients in Türkiye.

See our capabilities

  • Named Patient Access

    Bringing innovative medicines not yet licensed in Türkiye to individual patients through the named patient pathway.

  • Government & Institutional Supply

    Compliant supply to public institutions, hospitals and tenders, backed by reliable logistics.

  • Market Access & Reimbursement

    Türkiye-specific strategy for pricing, reimbursement and sustainable patient access.

  • Regulatory Pathway Support

    Guidance through the Turkish regulatory pathway, from documentation to approval.

  • Exclusive Distribution & Local Representation

    Acting as the manufacturer's committed local partner and representative in Türkiye.

  • Specialty & Rare Disease Medicines

    A focus on specialty and orphan therapies, where access matters most.

  • Direct Manufacturer-to-Patient Access

    Access solutions connecting manufacturers directly with patients through compliant channels.

From science to the patient

Bringing a therapy to patients moves through four stages, and we work alongside our partners at each one.

  1. Patient Access

    Connecting patients in need with innovative therapies.

  2. Market Access

    Opening the regulatory, pricing and reimbursement pathway.

  3. Supply

    Reliable, uninterrupted supply to institutions and patients.

  4. Local Partnership

    Long-term local representation and exclusive distribution.

Success should not be measured solely by how much a company grows, but by how many lives it helps bring closer to better treatment.
