About Us

We become the hope of patients by facilitating access to innovative medicines.

Orphion Pharma, as part of Orphion Group, is a young and dynamic company focused on facilitating international brands' access to the Turkish pharmaceutical market.

Orphion Pharma has two main business lines:

  • Supplying pharmaceutical products for direct supply programs, government tenders and prescription-based programs (Named Patient Program)
  • Consultancy and local representation for international companies planning to enter the Turkish pharmaceutical market

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the first address for international companies seeking cooperation in the Turkish pharmaceutical market and to increase patients' access to new original medicines and better generics.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create synergy by building sustainable relationships between international companies supplying pharmaceuticals in Türkiye and Turkish public authorities and pharmaceutical procurement institutions.

“The trusted brand in Türkiye’s pharmaceutical supply.” ~Orphion Pharma

Why Orphion Pharma?

Our Service at a Glance

Orphion Pharma collaborates with leading pharmaceutical companies worldwide. Thanks to our reliability and professionalism, we help international companies enter the Turkish market smoothly.
